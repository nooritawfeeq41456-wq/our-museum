How to visit

  • Walk: click or tap the floor. On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Look around: drag anywhere. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Photos: click a frame to add, replace or remove its photo, and to put roses or gold hearts around it. You both see changes right away.
  • Chat: type at the bottom or open Chat. Messages also pop up over your character.
  • Together: when you're both signed in, you each control your own character and see each other move.
  • Kiss: press Kiss and your character walks over, you lean in for a kiss, and hearts float up. It works on a bench too.
  • Keys: 1 wave, 2 dance, 3 sit, 4 love, 5 kiss.
